About Us

Preventive, solution-oriented and effective legal support — since 2014.

Founded in 2014 by attorneys Tuğba Tüfek and Taner Türkan, Tüfek & Türkan Law Firm set out with a service philosophy built on mutual trust and satisfaction — preventive, solution-oriented, fast and effective legal support. Within this approach, alongside legal support for following up and resolving disputes that arise, providing preventive and protective services against potential disputes and problems is our principle and priority.

Our firm provides natural and legal person merchants operating in various sectors with every kind of legal support they may need — from incorporation to liquidation — in their relations with their employees as well as customers, suppliers, consumers, and public or private institutions; and contributes to the efficiency and development of their operational and organisational structures.

Advising and supporting clients across various branches of law, our firm has a team and structure specialised in Labour and Social Security Law, Civil Law (Family and Inheritance), Commercial Law (commercial enterprises, companies, negotiable instruments), Law of Obligations (compensation, special obligations, lease), Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law (enforcement, bankruptcy, composition), Intellectual Property Law, Contracts, and the residence and work permits of foreigners.
