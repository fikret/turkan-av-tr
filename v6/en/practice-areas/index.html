Practice Areas

We provide advisory services and litigation across a wide range of legal fields.

Commercial Law

Advisory and dispute resolution across all stages of commercial relationships, including commercial enterprises, negotiable instruments and commercial contracts.

Corporate Law

Support for corporate structuring and governance, from incorporation to liquidation, mergers, acquisitions and share transfers.

Individual & Collective Labour Law

Advisory and litigation in employer–employee disputes, including employment contracts, reinstatement, severance and collective labour relations.

Social Security Law

Legal support in social security disputes, service determination, occupational accidents and occupational diseases.

Real Estate Law

Advisory and dispute resolution in title deeds, leases, urban transformation, construction contracts and real estate investments.

Intellectual & Industrial Property Law

Expert representation in the registration, protection and enforcement of trademarks, patents, designs and copyrights.

Civil Law (Family & Inheritance)

A sensitive, solution-oriented approach to divorce, custody, matrimonial property, guardianship and inheritance matters.

Law of Obligations (Compensation & Lease)

Legal support in compensation claims arising from contracts and torts, lease relationships and special obligations.

Enforcement & Bankruptcy Law

Effective representation of creditors and debtors in debt collection, attachment, bankruptcy and composition proceedings.

Immigration Law

Advisory on the legal status of foreigners in Türkiye, citizenship, deportation and international protection procedures.

Residence & Work Permits

Management of residence and work permit applications and renewals for foreign employees and their families.

Administrative & Tax Law

Annulment and full-remedy actions against administrative acts, and settlement and litigation in tax disputes.

Criminal Law

Diligent defence of the rights of suspects, defendants and victims at the investigation and prosecution stages.

Insurance Law

Representation of insured parties and insurers in insurance contracts, indemnity claims and recourse actions.

Data Protection Law

Advisory on KVKK and GDPR compliance, data inventories, disclosure and consent processes, and breach management.

Consumer Law

Legal support regarding defective goods and services, consumer contracts and arbitration committee / consumer court proceedings.

Contracts Law

Drafting, negotiation and risk analysis of commercial and private contracts to prevent disputes.
