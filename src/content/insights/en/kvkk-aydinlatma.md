The Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) imposes certain obligations on data controllers when processing personal data. Foremost among these is the **duty to inform**.

## What is the duty to inform?

When processing personal data, the data controller is obliged to inform the data subject about the purposes of processing, to whom and for what purposes the data may be transferred, and the method and legal basis of data collection.

## Practical recommendations

- Draft your privacy notices in clear, understandable and plain language.
- Keep your data processing inventory up to date.
- Separate processing activities that require explicit consent from those that do not.
